## Design purpose

The public site helps a guest understand the room, picture an event, explore real options and take the next step toward a visit or inquiry. It should feel warm, quiet, polished and trustworthy. The portal helps Arianna quickly handle leads, tours, follow-ups, proposals, payments, bills and events; it should feel calm, practical and fast. Treat the two as one journey with distinct visual contexts.

Use familiar controls, precise typography, restrained gold, generous but purposeful spacing and one clear primary action. Prefer real venue imagery and useful information to decorative cards, badges, glows, blur, tiny labels or generic copy. Borrow product principles, not another brand's appearance. Preserve existing features and do not invent prices, availability, proof or operational data.

## Public website

- **Shell and type:** Use the shared `(site)` layout, `Header`, `Footer`, and current font definitions. Editorial headings use Cormorant Garamond; body and controls use Manrope. Keep headline lines intentional, readable and centered when the design calls for it; do not force a word onto a new line while space is available. Eyebrows are occasional supporting labels, not a substitute for hierarchy. Keep public copy direct and use `Quinceañera` and `Quinceañeras` correctly.
- **Color and surfaces:** The default is a light warm ivory, cream and white palette, dark readable text, fine warm borders and restrained gold. The source can contain legacy dark class names remapped by `globals.css`; verify the computed result. Do not import portal dark surfaces. Any intentionally retained dark editorial section needs explicit, accessible light text.
- **Images and shape:** Feature the actual venue. Inset hero images, photo cards, lightboxes and public content panels should have visibly rounded corners consistent with `rounded-2xl`; clip images and overlays together. Use an intentional landscape crop for split heroes rather than an accidental square. Full-bleed mobile and tablet hero films are the exception: edge to edge with square edges. Preserve readable image overlays and accessible controls.
- **Responsive heroes:** Desktop can use a deliberate split hero with centered text within its column and a rounded adjacent image or film. For the established packages and gallery heroes, mobile and portrait tablet show the film edge to edge above centered introductory text; desktop shows the rounded film beside it. Avoid overflow, unexpected text wrapping and horizontal page scroll. Above-the-fold content must render immediately, without scroll-triggered `Reveal`.
- **Actions and links:** Use `luxor-gold-raised` for the main conversion action with white text and icons. Secondary buttons stay visibly interactive with restrained hover and keyboard-focus feedback. Header navigation and footer contact links use a delicate animated gold underline on hover and focus; the settled line must remain thin. Social icon hover is subtle. Keep links accurate: address to maps, email to mailto, and phone to the configured number. In-page links scroll smoothly to their target with a header offset and honor reduced motion.
- **Forms:** Use shared date and event-type controls where available. Labels, icons and errors must be clear; maintain the public field's existing border thickness while matching the portal's gold focus treatment. Preserve readable placeholder contrast, touch targets and iOS-friendly text sizes. Do not weaken consent, validation or submission behavior for a visual change.
- **Gallery:** Use the existing custom gallery and lightbox. Keep categories easy to scan, selection feedback smooth and stable, and the photo grid aligned without unused columns. The mobile layout uses its compact filter arrangement and photo carousel; changes in category should transition smoothly. Rounded inset images and desktop lightboxes contrast with edge-to-edge mobile hero film. Keep navigation, keyboard behavior, labels and photo descriptions intact.
- **Packages:** The configurator's empty summary should hold useful space with quiet placeholders. On the first calculation, transition into the result without a sudden size jump. During later option changes, retain the existing breakdown, animate added or removed rows in place, and subtly identify the newest selection. Keep totals accurate, indicate errors and avoid presenting stale amounts as confirmed. On mobile, the breakdown may expand and collapse; the total and next action remain easy to find. See `PackagesConfiguratorSummary.tsx` and its data flow before changing it.
- **Public Elena:** Preserve the existing concierge's actions and behavior. Its message flow, composer, cards and transitions should feel related to portal Elena but use the public site's light, warm palette. Keep the composer legible, focused state clear, and booking and brochure actions usable on mobile. Never add an assistant or automated action without a demonstrated need.

## Owner portal

- **Typography and density:** Use Manrope for operational headings, labels, buttons and numbers; reserve Cormorant for the Luxor wordmark and actual client-facing documents or previews. Use tabular numerals for amounts, counts, dates and percentages. Prefer sentence-case headings, useful context near the action, and fewer wrappers. Do not duplicate a person's name in both a task title and its supporting line.
- **Themes and components:** Use `--portal-*` tokens and the shared controls in `src/components/portal/PortalUI.tsx`. Test Arianna's light theme first and dark theme as well. Reuse `PortalButton`, `PortalSelect`, `PortalDatePicker`, `PortalModal`, close controls, animated tabs, transitions and pagination where they fit. Portal selects and calendars use the shared React controls. Match existing field border widths and focus treatment; check hover, disabled, selected, empty, loading and error states.
- **Navigation:** Use `PortalAnimatedTabs` with the slider variant for suitable desktop sub-navigation and `PortalTabTransition` for panel handoffs where appropriate. When a page has too many views, its established Browse views pattern uses horizontal tabs at desktop XL, a compact trigger on tablet and a full-width trigger on mobile, opening an accessible modal or drawer. Respect the page's existing interaction rather than imposing extra navigation on simple pages. Pagination shows labeled previous/next controls and the current page.
- **Task completion:** Reuse `PortalTaskCrossOff` for the established animated gold task-completion treatment. In calendar schedule views, retain completed tasks with their strikethrough for the day and clear them the next day; in overdue views, animate the cross-off and remove the completed item smoothly as following rows move up. Preserve the underlying scheduling behavior; do not delete tasks merely to produce an animation. Linked records should be clearly actionable without duplicate buttons.
- **Elena composer:** The `PortalElenaChat` trailing control remains a raised gold circle with white icon in send, start-voice and stop states. Stop uses a rounded white square, not an X. Keep the input, keyboard handling, response state and existing server authorization intact.
- **Popovers:** Where an established frosted portal popover is needed, use the shared `portal-popover-surface` and existing portal positioning pattern. A transformed ancestor can break backdrop blur: portal to the body, keep blur on a non-transformed shell, animate the outer layer's opacity, and place scrolling in an inner element. Check light and dark themes and computed `backdrop-filter`. Do not add glass effects to ordinary controls.

## Motion, expansion and loading

- Animate functional state changes in both directions: panels, accordions and containers expand and collapse without abrupt unmounts; adjacent content moves naturally. Drawers, dialogs, filters and tab panels enter and leave smoothly. Preserve focus, keyboard access, scroll position and rapid-toggle correctness. Match motion to the component; avoid decorative movement or delaying the primary task.
- Use existing Framer Motion and shared transition patterns before adding dependencies. Animate height or layout when content changes size; keep content mounted long enough for exit motion. Use `aria-expanded` and an associated controlled region for disclosures. Avoid clipping focus indicators or scrolling content while animating. The packages summary is a public reference; `PortalModal`, `PortalTabTransition` and `PortalSkeletonTransition` are portal references, not automatic wrappers for every element.
- Loading should stay local to the affected content. Match skeleton geometry to the eventual content and avoid layout shifts. Keep already available content visible during refreshes where it remains valid, and distinguish stale, loading and error states. Use subtle handoffs only when they improve comprehension; do not require blur everywhere.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: eliminate or greatly shorten movement and blur while preserving immediate state changes, functionality and focus. Avoid scroll-triggered reveals above the fold.
